A little more about me

I’m a practical, people-oriented problem solver who enjoys being where people, projects and the outdoors meet.

The common thread

I’ve worked in management, fire safety, hospitality, outdoor education, campsite operations and Christian youth ministry. Those roles look quite different on paper, but they have given me a common set of strengths: organising people, solving problems, communicating clearly, taking responsibility and staying calm when things don’t go according to plan.

Useful technology

I’m also increasingly interested in technology and software. I enjoy figuring out how things work, experimenting with Linux, building small tools and finding ways technology can make everyday work simpler.

I don’t necessarily want to build technology for technology’s sake. I like useful technology.

People

And I really enjoy working with people — especially developing young leaders and creating environments where people can learn, grow and have a good experience.

What I’m looking for

A working environment in a forward-looking organisation where I can be myself — and bring the best of what I can do. A place that values calm problem-solving, honest communication and real leadership as much as the bottom line.
