About Me

The story behind the CV

I built my career across industries that share one thing: real people, real places, and no room for sloppy work. Fire safety, school camps, guest farms and outdoor programmes all taught me the same lesson — good management is mostly good listening.

Who I am

To be honest, that question has been up for debate my whole career. I’ve done many different kinds of work in many different settings, and at times it has made me wonder where exactly I fit as a professional. The answer I’ve landed on: I am someone who can walk into almost any operation and make it run better. Given a little time to learn the ropes, I adapt — and I deliver.

The honest answer to the question above? I am still being shaped. It’s why I keep learning: my wish is to find a working environment, in a forward-looking country, where I can be who I am and bring the best of what I can do.

What I bring

Management is my first language — people management especially. I’ve run teams across budgets, vehicles, stockrooms and event schedules, and I’ve attended countless workshops and seminars on both management and leadership, because the two are genuinely different. One manages tasks; the other grows people. I try to do both.

What I’m looking for

A working environment in a forward-looking organisation where I can be myself — and bring the best of what I can do. A place that values calm problem-solving, honest communication and real leadership as much as the bottom line.

Xander Theron
